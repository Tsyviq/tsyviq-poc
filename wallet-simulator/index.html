BOD vs ROD

BOD — Bit-Origin Data
Anything created purely by digital operations: an intent, a policy result, a pending-request status. A compromised agent can produce as much BOD as it wants — BOD decides nothing by itself.
ROD — Reality-Origin Data
A digital trace whose valid appearance depends on a real physical event (here: a trusted USB insertion), acquired locally by Event Source. Not just "signed JSON" — its origin is what matters.
